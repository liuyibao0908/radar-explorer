06 / REAL-WORLD DETAILS

In Reality, Radar Has to Handle More Than One Echo

⏱

Clock Synchronization

The transmit and receive timestamps must come from the same time base. Any drift becomes a distance error.

µ

Atmospheric Refraction

Radio waves don't travel at exactly c through air. Humidity, temperature, and pressure all slow the wave slightly.

∞

Signal Processing

Real echoes are noisy. Filtering and correlation techniques pick the true round-trip time out of background noise.

⊕

Multiple Targets

Many echoes return at once. The radar separates them by time-of-arrival to map each to its own target.
