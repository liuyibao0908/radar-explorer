Can Radar Be Jammed?

Yes. That is the entire reason half of this website exists.

On this page

Yes. A radar can be jammed — and this is one of the most contested parts of electronic warfare.

There are two very different ways to attack a radar. One floods it with noise so the real echoes disappear underneath. The other sends it fake echoes that look exactly like real ones. Both work, and they are countered in completely different ways.

Noise jamming is brute force: raise the noise floor until the signal sits inside it. Deception is subtler — return a pulse at the right moment with the right shape, so the radar plots an aircraft that isn't there.

Every counter has a counter-counter. That is why radar and electronic warfare have advanced together for a century.

01 TWO KINDS OF ATTACK

Brawl Versus Bluff

One attack hides your signal. The other fabricates one.

02 NOISE JAMMING

Raising the Floor

You do not need to overpower the radar. You only need to be louder than the echoes.

Radar peak transmit power=+77 dBm
Return from a fighter at 200 km=−116 dBm
Thermal noise in that cell=−113 dBm
A 1 kW jammer only needs=about −100 dBm at the radar antenna
That is a margin of=16 dB over the signal — a 40× jammer
Jammer effective range=hundreds of km, because the path loss is shared

This is the asymmetry that makes noise jamming cheap: the radar has to win 190 dB of round-trip path loss before it sees anything, and the jammer only faces one-way loss.

03 ANIMATION: THE FLOOR RISES

Watch the Target Disappear Into Noise

The signal never changes. Only the floor underneath it does.

1. CLEAN -- the echo sits above the noise floor

2. JAMMED -- the floor rises and swallows the echo

3. FILTERED -- the radar narrows its bandwidth and recovers

4. RE-JAMMED -- the jammer moves to the new frequency

That last step is why counters and counter-counters exist: every defence narrows the frequency the attacker can use, and the attacker then has to find another one.

04 WORKED EXAMPLE

Why Weather Radar Is Hard to Jam

The same physics that makes a search radar vulnerable makes a weather radar awkward to attack.

A jammer would have to cover=a 400 km disc, continuously, in the right band
Available spectrum is=narrow — the S band is a small slice
Frequency agility defeats=a jammer tuned to one frequency
Striking radars are=hardened, shielded and self-protected
But weather data is public=no reason to attack — jamming it helps nobody
Result=weather radar is essentially never jammed

Countermeasures are aimed at radars whose output matters to somebody. A weather radar's output matters to everybody, which is its own kind of protection.

05 HOW RADAR FIGHTS BACK

Six Countermeasures

Each one attacks a specific weakness of a specific jammer.

FREQ

Frequency Agility

Change frequency between pulses. A jammer tuned to one frequency is now wasting its power, and it must retune fast enough to matter.

POWER

More Peak Power

Raise the signal above the floor. Simple and effective, but it costs size, cost and electricity — which is why power is a blunt counter.

FILTER

Narrow Bandwidth

Match the filter tightly to the transmitted pulse. Noise spread thinly across a wide band is cut hard; the signal inside the passband is not.

BEAM

Sidelobe Rejection

A jammer arriving off the main axis enters through the antenna's sidelobes. Directing the antenna at the jammer and nulling it is a well-established technique.

SPACE

Diverse Installations

Spread several radars around a site. A jammer close enough to silence one has to be silent against all of them at once.

HOME

Home on Jam

Locate the jammer directly, then turn the radar into a sensor that points at it and fires. The attacker's own transmitter becomes the target.

Notice that five of the six narrow what the attacker can do. Real radars combine several, because the jammer is not stupid.

06 DECEPTION

The Attack You Cannot Filter

Noise jamming loses to a filter. A well-formed false echo does not.

RANGE

Range Gate Stealing

The jammer receives the radar's pulse, delays it, and returns a stronger echo at a chosen range. The aircraft vanishes and a ghost appears behind it.

ANGLE

Angle Deception

By controlling the phase across a antenna array, a jammer can make the return appear to come from a different bearing entirely.

TRACK

False Tracks

Multiple false returns pulled slowly across the display form a plausible track with a plausible speed. The operator has to decide it is false.

TGT

False Targets

A drone that mimics a missile's radar signature is now a real and widely deployed threat — the deception idea has become physical.

TELL

Telling Them Apart

Deception is usually detected by inconsistencies: a track that appears from nowhere, moves at impossible speed, or appears from exactly the radar's own bearing.

LIMIT

The Fundamental Limit

Radar cannot prove an echo is genuine. It can only look for reasons to doubt it — which is why the operator stays in the loop.

Noise jamming asks the radar to see better. Deception asks it to doubt what it sees. The second question is much harder.

ONE-LINE SUMMARY

Yes — radar can be jammed, and no counter defeats every jammer.
