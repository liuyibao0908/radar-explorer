Why Can Radar See Aircraft?

By Editorial · Sept 19, 2026

A radar has no eyes. So how can it find aircraft in the sky?

ONE-LINE SUMMARY

01 / RADAR WAVE REFLECTION

How a Radar Wave Bounces Off an Aircraft

The radar sends out radio waves. These waves travel through the air and bounce off an aircraft. The wave that comes back is called an "echo."

02 / ANALOGY: ECHO IN A VALLEY

Like an Echo in a Valley

Think of shouting in a valley — when you hear an echo, you know there is a hill or a wall ahead. Radar works the same way.

=
03 / ANIMATION: SEND → REFLECT → RECEIVE → DECIDE

Watch the Radar Loop in Motion

Each radar cycle has four steps: send a wave, let it reflect off a target, receive the echo, and decide what was detected. The loop then repeats — many times per second.

04 / SIGNAL vs CLUTTER

Strong Echo vs Background Clutter

Weather, ground, and sea waves also reflect waves. They create "clutter" that the radar must filter out — only real targets like aircraft stay clear.

05 / PROCESS RECAP

The Radar Flow, in Four Steps

Whether you are detecting a single aircraft or scanning the whole sky, every radar cycle follows the same four-step flow.

  1. 01 SEND The radar emits a pulse of electromagnetic energy.
  2. 02 REFLECT The wave hits an object and part of the energy bounces back.
  3. 03 RECEIVE The radar antenna picks up the echo.
  4. 04 DECIDE Distance, direction and speed are measured and reported.
06 / LIMITATIONS

When Radar Cannot See

A radar doesn't just "see" — it also measures distance, direction, and speed. If a target is too small, too far, or reflects too weakly, the radar may not see it.

↘

Too Small

A tiny drone or a bird may not return enough echo to be detected at long range.

→

Too Far

Beyond the radar's maximum range, echoes become too weak to distinguish from noise.

≋

Too Weak

Materials that don't reflect radio waves well — like stealth coatings — can hide a target.

⚡

Buried in Clutter

If the echo is hidden inside strong clutter, the radar must filter very carefully to find it.

ONE-LINE SUMMARY

Radar finds aircraft by send-reflect-receive. The stronger the echo, the easier to find.
